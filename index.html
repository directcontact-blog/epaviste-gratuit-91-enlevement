🔍 Expert Technical Diagnosis

When a vehicle is abandoned on a public road in Melun, drivers notice a heavy, rusted mass that blocks traffic, emits foul odours and sometimes drips oil onto the pavement. Typical symptoms are a broken windscreen, crushed chassis, or a burnt engine that leaves smoke and an acrid smell. The root cause is usually a collision, illegal parking, or an unrepaired mechanical failure that rendered the car inoperable. Ignoring the wreck can lead to fines, increased accident risk, environmental contamination from leaking fluids, and a loss of road‑space for emergency services. The epaviste must first secure the site, then lift the vehicle with a hydraulic crane, disconnect the battery, and drain all hazardous liquids. Finally, a compliance certificate is issued, the chassis number is recorded, and the vehicle is transported to an authorised recycling centre, meeting French and EU waste‑management regulations.
